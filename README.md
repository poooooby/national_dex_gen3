# National Dex Gen 3 📖

*All 1025 Pokémon in FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald. Catch, train, evolve and
battle with every species from Turtwig to Pecharunt.*

</div>

A mod for [Gen1Recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp)'s Generation 3 games. Those
games know 386 Pokémon; this mod adds the other 639, so Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar and
Paldea Pokémon can live in Kanto and Hoenn.

## ✨ Features

### Core Mechanics

- 🆕 **639 new Pokémon** (#387–1025) that work like any other: catch, train, evolve, trade and
  battle with them. Each has its stats, typing, Pokédex entry and cry.
- 🔄 **New evolutions for old favourites:** Rhydon into Rhyperior, Magneton into Magnezone,
  Nosepass into Probopass, Scyther into Kleavor, Togetic into Togekiss and more.
- 💎 **The items to do it:** Dusk Stone, Protector, Dubious Disc and 35 more evolution items,
  sold in department stores and late-game marts.
- 🌍 **128 alternate forms:** Alolan, Galarian, Hisuian and Paldean forms (of the originals too,
  each evolving into its own region's form), Rotom's appliances, Wormadam's cloaks,
  Oricorio's styles, Alcremie's creams, Giratina Origin, Kyurem Black and many more, each a
  Pokémon of its own.

### Fits the Game

- ⚔️ **170 newer moves, for real:** Moonblast, Dazzling Gleam, Play Rough, Shadow Claw, Earth
  Power, Flash Cannon, Brave Bird, Aqua Jet and more, with their own power, accuracy, PP, priority,
  type, battle text and description, including new mechanics: Nasty Plot, Hone Claws, Work Up,
  Quiver Dance, Close Combat, Flame Charge, Lunge, Fire Fang, Flare Blitz, Draining Kiss, Shore Up
  and more. The rest (Stealth Rock, U-turn, Trick Room ...) are swapped, at the same level, for
  the closest Gen 3 move of the same type.
- 🧬 **52 newer abilities, for real:** Technician, Adaptability, Multiscale, Snow Warning,
  Regenerator, Prankster, Moxie, Sap Sipper, Iron Fist, Strong Jaw, Frisk and more work in
  battle, with their own name and description. Pokémon you already have get them too. Every
  other newer ability is the closest Gen 3 one.
- 🧚 **The Fairy type:** Fairy is a real type, with today's matchups (it beats Dragon, Dark and
  Fighting; Dragon can't touch it; Poison and Steel hit it hard) and its own type icon. Every
  Pokémon has its modern typing, the originals too: Clefairy, Jigglypuff, Togepi, Snubbull,
  Marill, Mr. Mime, Ralts and Mawile are part Fairy, and Charm, Sweet Kiss and Moonlight are
  Fairy moves. Steel no longer resists Ghost and Dark, as in today's games.
- 📖 **A bigger Pokédex:** The new Pokémon join the National Pokédex, with a FORMS page for
  species with alternate forms, and fast scrolling when you hold a direction.

## ⚙️ Options

Open **OPTIONS → MODS → National Dex Gen 3**.

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clock Source** | In-game / Device | In-game | Which clock time-of-day evolutions read (Midnight Lycanroc, Alcremie's night and day creams, Budew, Riolu, Chingling, Snom). FireRed and LeafGreen have no clock, so they always use the device's. |
| **National Dex** | On / Off | Off | Turns the National Pokédex on from the start, instead of earning it in the story. Nothing is written to your save. |
| **Register Owned** | On / Off | On | Opening the Pokédex counts every Pokémon in your party and PC as seen and caught, handy for Pokémon added with a save editor. |

## 💾 Installation

1. Import your own FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire or Emerald into
   [Gen1Recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp).
2. Download the latest `national_dex_gen3-….zip` from the
   [Releases](https://github.com/poooooby/national_dex_gen3/releases/latest) page.
3. In the game, go to **MODS → Import mod .zip** and choose the file.
4. Add the companion mods below and make sure all of them are on.

## 🔗 Works With

This mod adds the Pokémon. Two companions bring them to life:

- 🎞️ [G9 Battle Sprites (Gen 3)](https://github.com/poooooby/g9-battle-sprites-gen3): **the
  pictures.** Animated battle sprites, summary and Pokédex pictures and party icons. Without it,
  new Pokémon show a blank or garbled picture.
- 🗺️ [Modern Spawns](https://github.com/poooooby/g1r_modern_spawns): **the wild.** Fills every
  area with Pokémon from all generations. With it, the Pokédex can even show where to find a
  species you haven't seen yet.
- 🌿 [Wilds of Hoenn](https://github.com/poooooby/wilds-of-hoenn): see them roaming the overworld,
  each with its own sprite.

## 📌 Good to Know

- **Forms can't be found in the wild yet.** Gen 3 can't change a Pokémon's form, so they come
  from a save editor, plus a few that evolve into each other. Megas and Gigantamax forms aren't
  included.
- **Some evolutions are adapted.** Conditions Gen 3 can't check (a known move, rain, a held item
  at a time of day ...) get stand-ins, e.g. Piloswine → Mamoswine while knowing Rock Tomb, and
  Galarian Farfetch'd → Sirfetch'd after 3 critical hits in one battle.
- **TM, tutor and egg moves** are limited to what Gen 3 has.
- **The original 386 Pokémon,** the story, trainers and maps are unchanged, apart from the
  originals' Fairy typing and their new evolutions.
- **A few abilities work in part.** Leaf Guard, Sweet Veil and Flower Veil stop status moves but
  not a status an attack can cause; Super Luck doesn't stack with a high critical-hit move;
  Ice Body and Snow Cloak don't block hail damage.
- **Moves follow Gen 3's rules.** A move's type decides whether it is physical or special (Fairy
  moves are special, Shadow Claw is physical), and contest data for the newest moves is basic.
  They are learned by level-up and as egg moves; TMs and tutors are the original ones.
- **Removing this mod** from a save whose Pokémon know its moves or are its species breaks those
  Pokémon; keep the mod installed for that save.
- **Link play** expects both players to have the same type chart; trade and battle with
  players who use this mod too.
