# WoobleDex 📖

*All 1025 Pokémon in Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver, Crystal, FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby,
Sapphire and Emerald.*

</div>

A mod for [Gen1Recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp) that adds every Pokémon each game
is missing, up to Pecharunt, with today's moves, abilities, items and types to go with them.

## ✨ Features

### Core Mechanics

- 🆕 **Every Pokémon, #1–1025**, plus [128 alternate forms](docs/pokemon/forms.md)
- 🔄 **[Every evolution](docs/evolutions-and-forms.md)**, including newer ones for the originals
- ⚔️ **[Every newer move](docs/moves.md)** works for real
- 🧬 **[Every newer ability](docs/abilities.md)** works for real
- 🧚 **[The Fairy type](docs/fairy.md)** and today's type chart
- 📖 **The Pokédex runs to #1025**, with a FORMS page for alternate forms

### Items and Shopping

- 🏪 **Vending machines** in the department store sell [the new items](docs/items.md) and [TMs](docs/tms.md)
- 🎒 **Modern held items**, evolution items, Linking Cord and Exp. Candies
- 🍓 **EV-lowering berries** (Pomeg, Kelpsy, Qualot, Hondew, Grepa, Tamato) lower an EV and raise friendship in every Gen 3 game

### In Battle

- 🎬 **Animations from the Gen 9 Move Animation Project** for the newer moves and the original ones (FireRed to Emerald)
- 🏷️ **Type badges** beside each HP box
- 📊 **Stat Hints** show each stat change and what it multiplies by ("ATK 2x")

### Quality of Life

- 📦 **Bigger PC**: 40 boxes
- 🎒 **Bigger Bag**: 300 kinds of items in the Bag and in the PC
- 🌅 **Day and night** tint the outdoors by time of day
- 🆙 **Modern Moves**: the original Pokémon learn their moves at today's levels
- 📋 **Register Owned**: your party and PC count toward the Pokédex
- 🏆 **33 achievements** with Recomp Achievements

### Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver and Crystal

All of the above, plus:

- 🌿 **Natures** and **abilities** for every Pokémon
- 🌦️ **Weather and terrain**
- ⚖️ **Modern battle rules** in Red, Blue and Yellow (no 1-in-256 misses, today's critical hits)
- 📈 **Separate Sp. Atk and Sp. Def** in Red, Blue and Yellow
- ♂️ **Gender, shinies, held items and breeding** in Red, Blue and Yellow

## ⚙️ Options

Pick **WOOBLEDEX** in the game's **OPTIONS** menu. Each setting applies to every game it appears in.

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clock Source** | In-game / Device | In-game | Which clock Day & Night and time-of-day evolutions use (FireRed and LeafGreen always use the device) |
| **National Dex** | On / Off | Off | Unlocks the National Pokédex from the start |
| **Day & Night** | On / Off | On | Tints the outdoors for morning, evening and night |
| **Modern Moves** | On / Off | On | The original Pokémon learn moves as they do today; nothing they know is lost when off |
| **Bigger PC** | On / Off | On | 40 PC boxes; turning it off hides the extra boxes without losing them |
| **Bigger Bag** | On / Off | On | Room for 300 kinds of items; turning it off loses nothing |
| **Register Owned** | On / Off | On | Counts your party and PC as caught in the Pokédex |
| **Type Badges** | On / Off | On | Type badges beside each HP box |
| **Stat Hints** | On / Off | On | Stat changes beside each HP box |
| **Modern Rules** | On / Off | On | Red, Blue and Yellow only: today's battle rules |
| **Move Split** | On / Off | Off | Physical or special by the move itself, as in today's games, instead of by its type |

## 💾 Installation

1. Use [Gen1Recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp) **0.3.95 or newer** with your own game imported.
2. Download the latest zip from [Releases](https://github.com/poooooby/national_dex_gen3/releases/latest).
3. In the game, go to **MODS → Import mod .zip** and choose the file.
4. Add the companion mods below.

## 📌 Good to Know

- **WoobleDex was once called National Dex Gen 3.** Saves and settings carry over.
- **On Red to Crystal it replaces the original National Dex mod.** Turn that one off.
- **Alternate forms aren't found in the wild yet,** and there are no Megas or Gigantamax forms.
- **A few evolutions are adapted** where the game can't check a condition ([details](docs/evolutions-and-forms.md)).
- **A move's type decides physical or special,** as in the original games, unless you turn on **Move Split**. Link battles always use the game's own rule, so both players' games agree.
- **Keep the mod installed** on any save that uses its Pokémon or moves.
- **Exporting to a cartridge .sav** can't hold the new Pokémon, moves, items or extra boxes. Your mod save always keeps them.
- **Link play** works with players who also use WoobleDex.
- **The included save editor** knows everything WoobleDex adds.
