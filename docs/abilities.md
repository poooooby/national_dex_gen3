# Added Abilities

Abilities from later generations that work in battle here. Every other newer ability is the
closest Gen 3 ability (see each Pokémon's page). The summary screen has room for one short line,
so **What it does** below is the full effect, with every number and anything that works
differently from the newer games. Gen 3 decides physical or special by a move's type.

| Ability | Summary screen | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Adaptability | Powers up same-type moves. | STAB moves deal 2x instead of the usual 1.5x. |
| Aftermath | Hurts the foe as it faints. | If it faints from a contact move, the attacker loses 1/4 of its max HP. |
| Anticipation | Senses the foe's danger. | On entering battle it shudders if the foe knows a damaging move that is super effective against it. |
| Beast Boost | KOs raise its best stat. | When it knocks out a foe with a move, its highest stat (not counting HP) rises one stage. |
| Big Pecks | Prevents DEFENSE reduction. | Foes can't lower its DEFENSE. |
| Bulletproof | Blocks ball and bomb moves. | Ball and bomb moves have no effect on it: Barrage, Egg Bomb, Shadow Ball, Sludge Bomb, Zap Cannon, Octazooka, Mist Ball, Ice Ball, Bullet Seed, Weather Ball, Energy Ball, Focus Blast, Gyro Ball, Seed Bomb, Mud Bomb, Magnet Bomb, Rock Wrecker, Aura Sphere, Acid Spray, Searing Shot, Electro Ball, Pollen Puff, Beak Blast, Pyro Ball. |
| Competitive | Stat cuts raise SP. ATK. | When a foe lowers one of its stats, its SP. ATK rises two stages. |
| Defiant | Stat cuts raise ATTACK. | When a foe lowers one of its stats, its ATTACK rises two stages. |
| Dry Skin | Likes water, hates fire. | Water moves have no effect on it and restore 1/4 of its max HP instead. Fire moves deal 1.25x damage to it. In rain it restores 1/8 of its max HP each turn; in harsh sunlight it loses 1/8 each turn. |
| Filter | Dulls super-effective hits. | Super-effective moves deal 3/4 damage to it. |
| Flower Veil | Guards GRASS types' stats. | If it is a Grass type, foes can't lower its stats and status moves can't give it a status problem. |
| Fluffy | Contact halved; FIRE x2. | Moves that make contact deal half damage to it, but Fire moves deal double. A Fire move that makes contact (Fire Punch, Flare Blitz) deals normal damage. |
| Frisk | Checks the foe's held item. | On entering battle it reveals the foe's held item. |
| Healer | No effect in this game. | No effect here. In the newer games it may cure a battle partner's status problem; this game does not use it, even in double battles. |
| Heatproof | Halves FIRE-type damage. | Fire moves deal half damage to it. Burn damage is not halved here. |
| Honey Gather | No effect in this game. | No effect here. In the newer games it may pick up Honey after battle, an item Gen 3 does not have. |
| Hydration | Heals status in rain. | In rain, it is cured of a status problem (burn, poison, paralysis, sleep, freeze) at the end of the turn. |
| Ice Body | Slight HP recovery in hail. | Restores 1/16 of its max HP at the end of each turn in hail. Hail damage itself only spares Ice types here. |
| Iron Barbs | Hurts the foe on contact. | A foe that hits it with a contact move loses 1/8 of its max HP. |
| Iron Fist | Powers up punching moves. | Punching moves get 1.2x power (Mach Punch, Fire/Ice/Thunder Punch, Drain Punch, Bullet Punch, Sky Uppercut, Meteor Mash and the rest). |
| Justified | Ups ATTACK if hit by DARK. | Its ATTACK rises one stage when a Dark move hits it. |
| Leaf Guard | No status problems in sun. | In harsh sunlight, status moves can't give it a status problem. A damaging move's added effect (a burn from Flamethrower) still can. |
| Motor Drive | ELECTRIC moves raise SPEED. | Electric moves have no effect on it and raise its SPEED one stage instead. |
| Moxie | KOs raise ATTACK. | Its ATTACK rises one stage when it knocks out a foe with a move. |
| Multiscale | Less damage at full HP. | At full HP it takes half damage. |
| Poison Touch | May poison on contact. | Its contact moves have a 30% chance to poison the target. |
| Prankster | Status moves go first. | Its status moves get +1 priority, so they usually go first. Dark types are not immune to them here. |
| Prism Armor | Dulls super-effective hits. | Super-effective moves deal 3/4 damage to it. |
| Protosynthesis | Sun ups its best stat. | In harsh sunlight its highest stat goes up: 1.3x for ATTACK, DEFENSE, SP. ATK or SP. DEF, 1.5x for SPEED. Gen 3 has no Booster Energy, so sunlight is the only trigger. |
| Quark Drive | Electric field ups a stat. | On Electric Terrain its highest stat goes up: 1.3x for ATTACK, DEFENSE, SP. ATK or SP. DEF, 1.5x for SPEED. Gen 3 has no Booster Energy, so the terrain is the only trigger. |
| Rattled | BUG/GHOST/DARK hits up SPD. | Its SPEED rises one stage when a Bug, Ghost or Dark move hits it. |
| Reckless | Powers up recoil moves. | Recoil moves get 1.2x power (Double-Edge, Take Down, Submission, Volt Tackle, Brave Bird, Flare Blitz, Wood Hammer, Head Smash, Wild Charge, Head Charge, Wave Crash), and so do Jump Kick and High Jump Kick. |
| Regenerator | Recovers HP on switch-out. | Restores 1/3 of its max HP when it switches out (not when it faints). |
| Rivalry | Same gender up, other down | 1.25x power against a foe of the same gender, 0.75x against the other gender. No change if either is genderless. |
| Sand Force | Powers up moves in sand. | In a sandstorm, Rock, Ground and Steel moves get 1.3x power. It does not block sandstorm damage here; only the types and Sand Veil that Gen 3 already spares are safe. |
| Sand Rush | Ups SPEED in a sandstorm. | Its SPEED doubles in a sandstorm. It does not block sandstorm damage here. |
| Sap Sipper | GRASS moves raise ATTACK. | Grass moves have no effect on it and raise its ATTACK one stage instead. |
| Slush Rush | Ups SPEED in hail. | Its SPEED doubles in hail. |
| Sniper | Powers up critical hits. | Its critical hits deal a further 1.5x (3x in all, since a Gen 3 critical hit is 2x). |
| Snow Cloak | Ups evasion in hail. | In hail, moves aimed at it are 20% less accurate. Hail damage itself only spares Ice types here. |
| Snow Warning | Summons hail in battle. | Starts hail when it enters battle. The hail lasts until other weather replaces it, like Drizzle and Drought in Gen 3. |
| Solid Rock | Dulls super-effective hits. | Super-effective moves deal 3/4 damage to it. |
| Stamina | Ups DEFENSE when hit. | Its DEFENSE rises one stage every time a damaging move hits it. |
| Storm Drain | WATER moves raise SP. ATK. | Water moves have no effect on it and raise its SP. ATK one stage instead. |
| Strong Jaw | Powers up biting moves. | Biting moves get 1.5x power: Bite, Crunch, Hyper Fang, Poison Fang, Fire Fang, Ice Fang, Thunder Fang, Psychic Fangs, Jaw Lock, Fishious Rend. |
| Super Luck | Raises critical-hit ratio. | Its moves land critical hits more often: one stage up, as if every move had a high critical-hit ratio. |
| Sweet Veil | Prevents sleep. | It can't be put to sleep by a move. |
| Technician | Powers up weak moves. | Moves with 60 power or less get 1.5x power. |
| Telepathy | No effect in this game. | No effect here. In the newer games it avoids a battle partner's attacks; this game does not use it, even in double battles. |
| Tinted Lens | Boosts resisted moves. | Its not-very-effective moves deal double damage. |
| Tough Claws | Powers up contact moves. | Moves that make contact get 1.3x power. |
| Weak Armor | Physical hits: -DEF, +SPEED | When a physical move hits it, its DEFENSE falls one stage and its SPEED rises two. |
