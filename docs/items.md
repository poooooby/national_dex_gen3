# Added items

WoobleDex adds 39 evolution items that no Gen 3 game has, so the Pokémon that
need them can evolve. They are all sold by the EVO EXPRESS vending machine: Celadon Dept. Store
4F in FireRed / LeafGreen, Lilycove Dept. Store 2F in Ruby / Sapphire / Emerald.

Further down: [other items](#other-items), the Exp. Candies and the Dynamax items, and the
[held items](#held-items).

*Generated from `data/items.lua`, `data/shops.lua`, `data/vending.lua`, the Exp. Candy and Dynamax item lists by
`tools/build_docs.lua`.*

Prices are what a store charges; it buys the item back at half. They come from Bulbapedia
(twice the sell price listed for the earliest game that has one), or ₽2100, the Gen 3
evolution stone price, where there is none. See [CREDITS](../CREDITS.md).

Badges is how many Gym Badges the machine wants before it sells the item, after where the
item turns up in the newest game that has Gym Badges; post-game finds need all 8.

### MAX HONEY

- Price: ₽3000
- Not used on its own: Urshifu's Max Soup takes 1 Max Honey along with the 3 Max Mushrooms.

### DYNAMAX CRYSTL

- Price: ₽10000
- No use from the Bag: Dynamax Raids spends one at a den for a legendary or mythical Pokémon's raid.
